How to Break into Strategy Consulting

Here are some practical steps to land your first role or internship in strategy consulting:

  • Build a strong resume with leadership roles and quantifiable impact
  • Network with alumni and recruiters on LinkedIn
  • Join consulting clubs and participate in case competitions
  • Practice frameworks like Porter's 5 Forces, 4Ps, and Profitability Trees
  • Use platforms like CaseCoach and Victor Cheng's CaseInterview.com

Resume Tips Comparison

Common Mistake Better Version
“Helped with project” “Led a team of 4 to complete market entry analysis in 3 weeks”
“Responsible for managing data” “Analyzed 10,000+ data points to improve efficiency by 15%”

Resume Tips

Use action verbs, show results (numbers!!), and tailor to each firm.

Interview Prep

Practice case studies and behavioral questions weekly.

Networking

Reach out to consultants with specific questions, not generic messages.
