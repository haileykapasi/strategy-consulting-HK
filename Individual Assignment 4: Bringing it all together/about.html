What is Strategy Consulting?

Strategy consulting focuses on helping organizations make high-level decisions to achieve long-term goals. Consultants work with top executives to solve big-picture problems like market entry, competitive advantage, M&A, and profitability.

Key Firms: McKinsey & Co., BCG, Bain & Co. (MBB)

Strategy Consulting

Focuses on high-level decisions, growth, and long-term planning.

Management Consulting

More operational, improving processes, teams, or efficiency.

IT / Tech Consulting

Helps firms adopt and implement technology solutions.
